# AVMSD delivery and evidence repository

This repository helps Double FW Ltd and authorised collaborators plan audiovisual accessibility, follow delivery to viewers, measure improvement and prepare evidence. Its main workstream is Article 7 of amended Directive 2010/13/EU. Seven guides connect editorial decisions, access features, distribution, viewer outcomes and reporting. Wider AVMSD duties have explicit specialist routes; those routes do not constitute complete assessment of every obligation.

**Adoption state: draft for competent human review.** Structural validation and synthetic developer exercises can be completed without approving a real service. No real national adapter, provider classification, regulatory report, participant study or public compliance claim is approved by this package. The example's people, country, obligations and observations are fictional. No broadcast chain, regulator portal or orchestration integration is implemented.

## Bootstrap
1. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for authority and execution rules.
2. Inspect the [document index](control/document-index.yaml), [source register](control/source-register.yaml), [source review](control/source-review.md) and [dated legal status](control/legal-status.yaml).
3. Establish the service, entity, jurisdiction, dates, programme versions, distribution routes and actual delegation. Record missing context.
4. Select a [workflow](workflows/workflows.yaml) and retrieve only the indexed guide sections and necessary source units.
5. Instantiate [records](records/README.md), preserve evidence and run the [validator](validation/README.md) before review.

## Authority and limits
Law, national rules and licence conditions need precise dated provisions. Double-FW production and research material supplies recommended practice; its recommendations do not become EU quotas. Repository rules govern record integrity and automation only after adoption. Interpretations require accountable human review. Synthetic examples and historical sources are excluded from real legal evidence and active requirement retrieval respectively.

For a blocked dependency, record affected action, owner, reason, next evidence and resume condition. Continue independent drafting, inventory or validation; never invent missing national rules, approvals or technical results. The [validation report](control/validation-report.md) separates executed checks from pending legal, editorial, technical and independent evaluation. No repository-wide licence is selected: retain source attribution and confirm reuse rights before redistribution or training.

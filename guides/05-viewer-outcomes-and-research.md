# Viewer outcomes and research

## Purpose and trigger
Evaluate whether intended viewers can understand, control and enjoy the delivered experience. Trigger discovery research before creative decisions become fixed, formative evaluation during development, and final viewing studies after conventional barriers have been addressed. The accountable research owner approves methods and permissions; the service owner funds correction. Inputs include programme purpose, feature/language matrix, known barriers, delivered versions, research question and lawful data-handling plan.

## Select outcomes without rewriting their IDs
Use D08, the Inclusive Digital Service Outcome Dataset version 1.0, as an organisational research framework. Preserve its canonical IDs. It is not an AVMSD statutory list or a substitute for technical evaluation. The selected viewing measures should connect to a real decision and retain the dataset's definitions and misuse warnings.

| Viewing question | Relevant IDSO records |
| --- | --- |
| Can viewers perceive and understand essential meaning? | IDSO-01, IDSO-02, IDSO-05 |
| Can they find, control, resume and recover the experience? | IDSO-03, IDSO-04, IDSO-06, IDSO-11, IDSO-15 |
| Can they complete accurately with reasonable effort and autonomy? | IDSO-09, IDSO-10, IDSO-12 |
| Are privacy, equivalence and dignity protected? | IDSO-13, IDSO-14, IDSO-17 |
| Are confidence, satisfaction and material enjoyment supported? | IDSO-16, IDSO-18, IDSO-20 |
| Can they report a barrier or obtain support? | IDSO-08 |

Confirm the exact IDSO definitions before instantiating a measure. A comprehension task measures an observed response; satisfaction and confidence are reported judgements. Neither supplies the other. Include narrative meaning, factual accuracy, timing of reveals, assistance, recovery and relevant enjoyment rather than asking only whether viewers liked the feature.

## Recruit beyond current successful viewers
Recruit people whose access approaches, languages and combined needs match the questions, including people excluded from registration, feature discovery or complaint channels. Use voluntary overlapping functional-needs lenses rather than diagnostic assumptions. Include relevant sign-language users, blind/partially sighted viewers, Deaf/hard-of-hearing viewers and combined needs as justified by scope. Broader cognitive or interaction research is labelled practice, not an invented AVMSD audience taxonomy.

Choose sample and method for the decision, consequences and uncertainty. Do not invent a universal participant count or task-time threshold. Make invitations, consent, compensation arrangements, sessions and instruments accessible. Participants should be able to pause or withdraw without losing entitlement. Do not expose people to unsafe flashing content to validate a suspected technical defect.

## Define and validate measures
Each measure records definition, numerator/denominator where appropriate, unit or scale, task/context, method, instrument/version, provider/export contract, missing-data treatment, uncertainty and interpretation limits. Trial questions and exports before analysis; confirm response codes, branching, units and accessibility. Separate failed contact, non-response, abandonment and ineligible entries. Retain severe minority barriers and contradictory findings instead of smoothing them into an average.

Use qualitative accounts to explain mechanism and meaning. Quantitative comparisons need compatible tasks, populations, methods, period/version and appropriate uncertainty. Distinguish paired from independent observations and avoid causal or population claims unsupported by design. Small cells can expose identities; protect disclosure and do not equate suppressed detail with no barrier. Missing viewers or an inaccessible survey limit satisfaction estimates.

## Protect evidence and interpret carefully
Collect the minimum lawful data for the decision. Record access permissions, retention, purpose and model-use restrictions. D10 supports insight without disability surveillance; it does not establish a legal basis. The privacy owner verifies actual legal authority, special-category treatment and safeguards where relevant. Do not reuse children's protection data commercially; route Articles 6a/28b issues to Guide 07. Research consent does not automatically permit public AI training or unrestricted redistribution.

Synthetic scenarios help rehearse tasks and identify hypotheses. They are never participant evidence, lived experience or measured satisfaction. Keep them in a separate dataset and label fictional identities and observations. Operational personal evidence remains outside the public repository.

## Outputs, decisions and next workflow
Output viewer findings using the IDSO vocabulary, protected evidence, limitations and correction recommendations. Supported findings remain bounded by people, tasks, version and context. A positive study cannot cancel a missed legal target; a severe barrier prompts investigation even with a positive average. Stop unsupported generalisation, prohibited data reuse and unsafe sessions. Continue permitted analysis of independent evidence. Route issues to WF11, provision/progress questions to WF08 and legal implications to WF03/WF09. Monitor repeated viewing and changed content through WF12.

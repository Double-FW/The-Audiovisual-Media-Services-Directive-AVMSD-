# Service scope and jurisdiction

## Purpose and trigger
Establish which service, legal entity and national framework require assessment. Use this guide for a new service, acquisition, territory, editorial model or corporate restructuring, and when an existing classification becomes uncertain. The accountable human is the provider's designated legal decision owner. Agents collect facts and prepare alternatives; the owner approves classification and jurisdiction.

The inputs are the legal entity and group structure, head-office and editorial-decision locations, workforce facts, service description, intended public audience, economic model, schedule/catalogue organisation, distribution arrangements and relevant dates. A UK address or an EU viewer does not settle jurisdiction. Record absent facts as dependencies with an owner and a precise evidence request.

## Classify the activity
Apply Article 1 definitions to the actual activity. An editorial audiovisual media service offers programmes to inform, entertain or educate the general public through electronic communications networks under a provider's editorial responsibility. Assess economic character, principal purpose or a separately identifiable section, and effective control over selection and organisation. Free access does not automatically remove economic character.

Distinguish linear television, where a schedule enables simultaneous viewing, from on-demand viewing chosen from a provider's catalogue. A video-sharing platform organises programmes or user-generated videos without editorial responsibility for that content; the principal-purpose, separate-section or essential-function routes require separate analysis. Commission guidance G01 supports the essential-function assessment, without replacing the legal test.

A mixed business needs separate classification records for its platform and editorial service. A professional creator's channel may need its own assessment; the host's classification does not decide it. Incidental video, private communications and audio-only podcasts should be considered against the definitions rather than treated as universal exempt labels. Record other relevant legal routes even where AVMSD scope is not established.

## Establish jurisdiction and national rules
Use Articles 2–4 for editorial media services and Article 28a for platforms. Document the relevant establishment factors, including split head-office/editorial-workforce arrangements and any applicable subsidiary or group route. Where necessary, investigate the specified satellite connection rules. Do not reduce these tests to audience location or registration alone. Obtain competent legal interpretation for cross-border conflicts and circumvention questions.

The country-of-origin framework differs from specified targeting-based financial contribution provisions under Article 13. Record each affected obligation and country separately. A contribution in a targeted country does not transfer every service duty there. Check national rules that are stricter or more detailed within the EU framework, the competent regulator, service registration and any licence conditions.

## Build the jurisdiction adapter
Create a jurisdiction record containing the country, legal entity, service IDs, classification rationale, applicable periods, national acts and exact provisions, regulator, licence, reporting instructions, target calculation methods, exceptions and review triggers. Preserve original legal wording or a protected locator, currency checks and an interpretation record. Identify the duty holder and legal decision authority for every adopted obligation.

No real jurisdiction was supplied for this repository. Its templates provide the field contract; the fictional example adapter is labelled synthetic and cannot establish a real duty. A real adapter remains blocked until national primary sources, service facts and competent human approval are available. Do not fill the gap using another country's quota or a remembered reporting deadline.

## Evidence, decisions and next workflow
Output linked classification and jurisdiction records, a national-source gap list and an approved scope decision where supported. Hold scope findings separately from any limitation or proportionality rationale. Small size, live delivery and an editorial decision about AD are not blanket Article 7 exemptions. The EAA microenterprise and WAD live-media provisions do not supply AVMSD exceptions.

Stop the affected legal conclusion if jurisdiction, editorial control, national rules or decision authority remain unresolved. Continue asset inventory, draft production planning and synthetic validation. Changes to the evidence reopen scope; silence cannot approve it. Route established scope to WF03 for applicable requirements, and mixed or wider duties to Guide 07. Reassess classification before transferring an old decision to a new service or reporting period.

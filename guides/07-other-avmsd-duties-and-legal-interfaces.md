# Other AVMSD duties and legal interfaces

## Purpose and trigger
Route obligations outside the main accessibility workstream without claiming complete AVMSD assurance. Trigger this guide at scope intake, catalogue/content-policy change, a new commercial model, platform feature change or a legal update. The accountable legal owner assigns specialist module owners. Inputs are classification, jurisdiction, current national rules, licence, audiences, business model and data/commercial-content flows.

The matrix provides navigation, not a complete assessment checklist. Each routed module needs exact provisions, applicability, competent assessment methods and evidence. Unknown national details remain blocked. Approval of accessibility guidance does not approve child-protection, advertising or European-works compliance.

Keep linear provisions within their actual service and programme conditions. A professional channel and an editorial catalogue hosted on a platform may create different obligations from the platform's hosting activity. Do not apply Article 7 to every upload or infer a general duty to pre-approve all content. Platform measures require the specific legal framework, proportionality and rights safeguards, not an invented unlimited monitoring obligation.

## Preserve numerical and data distinctions
Article 13's at-least-30% on-demand European-works share and prominence concern cultural programming, not access-feature provision. Its low-turnover/audience and nature/theme routes concern specified obligations and cannot become general Article 7 exemptions. G02 is interpretative calculation guidance. A full module must verify definitions, national counting and relevant contributions, including targeting provisions.

The Article 23 television advertising/teleshopping spot limit is 20% in each of the specified 06:00–18:00 and 18:00–24:00 periods with defined exclusions; do not copy them into on-demand accessibility calculations. Sponsorship and product placement have separate conditions. Use specialist assessment rather than treating all promotional content as one interchangeable category.

Children's data collected or generated through relevant protection measures must not be repurposed commercially under Articles 6a/28b. Keep protection data separate from advertising, profiling, audience research and training pipelines. The relevant privacy owner must verify lawful processing and safeguards, rather than assuming an internal analytics permission overrides the statutory restriction.

## Assess interfaces through separate legal records
AVMSD audiovisual content/service duties differ from EAA duties for covered services providing access to audiovisual media and WAD duties for qualifying public websites/apps. Establish each law's scope and exclusions separately, including the WAD public-service-broadcaster exclusion. Shared delivery tests can support distinct requirement records; WCAG or EN 301 549 does not automatically establish Article 7 conformity. Do not import EAA CE marking, microenterprise provisions or a WAD statement template into AVMSD.

Assess EMFA and DSA interfaces only where the classified service and function require them. L03 Articles 28–29 removed AVMSD Article 30b with effect from 8 February 2025; record current Media Board arrangements rather than unchanged ERGA governance. Commission review activity in 2026 is a monitoring trigger, not adopted law. Article 7b does not supply a blanket prohibition on accessibility features or legitimate user choices; verify national integrity measures and recital 26 context.

## Outputs, decisions and next workflow
Output a routed requirement inventory, specialist ownership, evidence plan and explicit gaps. Stop a whole-AVMSD assurance claim while any relevant module remains unassessed. Continue independent accessibility delivery. Use WF02/WF03 for classification and requirements, WF01 for legal-source conflict resolution and WF12 for changes to conclusions or claims. Only the named authorised human can approve a provider-specific conclusion after the applicable specialist evidence is reviewed.

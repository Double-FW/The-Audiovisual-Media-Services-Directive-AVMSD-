# Accessibility duties and progress

## Purpose and trigger
Translate the applicable accessibility framework into a measurable plan without confusing ambition with delivery. Trigger this guide after scope approval, before a reporting period, or when national requirements, service provision or evidence changes. The provider's accountable service owner owns resources and implementation; the legal owner approves national interpretation. Inputs include classification, jurisdiction, current law, regulator instructions, catalogue/schedule inventory, feature baselines and known barriers.

## Article 7 routing
Article references are to amended Directive 2010/13/EU. Article 1(11) of 2018/1808 replaced Article 7. Consult L01/L02 and the current national adapter before a provider decision.

| Provision | Duty and evidence route |
| --- | --- |
| 7(1) | Member States ensure continuous, progressive accessibility through proportionate measures without undue delay. Link implemented measures and remaining barriers to the provider's applicable national duties. |
| 7(2), first sentence | Member States ensure regular provider reporting to national regulators. Obtain the actual provider timetable and method. |
| 7(2), second sentence | Member States report to the Commission from 19 December 2022 and every three years thereafter. This is a different duty holder and timetable. |
| 7(3) | Member States encourage provider action plans; any such plan is communicated to the national authority. Check additional national requirements. |
| 7(4) | Member States designate one easily accessible online information/complaints contact point. Identify it separately from the provider's channel. |
| 7(5) | Member States ensure accessible public emergency information through audiovisual services. Use Guide 06 for preparedness and urgent correction. |

The EU provision does not supply universal subtitle, signing, AD, accuracy, latency or provider reporting thresholds. A proposed organisational target belongs in a policy target record with its owner and rationale. National quotas, quality rules and licence conditions need their exact provisions, effective dates, population and calculation method. Do not infer a presumption of conformity from WCAG or EN 301 549.

## Plan measurable improvement
Use an inventory of exact editions, languages, durations, live/recorded status, regions and delivery routes. Establish a separate baseline for each access feature. Record the accessible minutes or qualifying titles, eligible denominator, repeat treatment, acquisitions, exclusions and failed or missing features. Distinguish intended, produced, supplied and tested usable provision. A feature in the master cannot establish the usable numerator.

Set actions with owners, resources, dependencies, due dates and success evidence. Prioritise consequences and persistent exclusion as well as volume. An action plan should explain how commissioning, acquisition, supplier correction, delivery reliability and viewer findings change the baseline. A promise or allocated budget is implementation preparation; it is not evidence that viewers received improvement.

Coverage uses the national counting method where applicable. The structured coverage record makes units and populations explicit, checks overlapping intervals and prevents a numerator exceeding its denominator. Keep separate calculations for SDH, AD, signing and spoken subtitles. Adding their percentages would double count content and imply equivalence between different access needs.

## Interpret five separate findings
Maintain applicability, provision, quality/delivery, viewer outcome and progress separately. A satisfied audience cannot cancel a missed legal target; a target met cannot cancel incorrect caption meaning. Changes in catalogue size can reduce a percentage despite increased accessible minutes. Compare periods only after reconciling populations, edits, durations, rights, repeat rules and method versions. State both absolute change and the relevant percentage.

An improvement finding needs baseline and current evidence plus a reasoned comparison. Use improved, unchanged, worsened or cannot compare; explain mixed feature or audience results. Do not label a process proven from a single completed cycle. Pilot repeated use, independent review, escaped-barrier monitoring and sustained correction first.

## Outputs and escalation
Output requirement and target records, per-feature baselines, an action plan, coverage calculations and scoped conclusions. The requirement register supplies EU routing; national fields remain blocked until verified. Agents may calculate and identify anomalies under delegation. Humans approve interpretation, proportionality decisions and conclusions.

Stop a target claim when counting rules, population reconciliation, national authority or usable-delivery evidence is missing. Preserve failed measures and assign the next evidence needed. Route the approved plan to WF04/WF05, calculations to WF08, and reporting to WF09. Monitor every plan after content, technology, supplier or national-rule changes through WF12.
